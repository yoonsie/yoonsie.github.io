Ruminations and remnants / a world for women

a world for women

passes tales of the world women found \ from start to finish

in a tongue with pronouns \ defaulted to women

designs tools to fit our hands \ and not for man

with no blame assigned to those \ who don’t fit this mold

and shares common knowledge \ that man starts from woman,

from womb or fetal form \ start from xx chromosome

it's all known, a woman is not a mini-man \ or less than

and not borne of rib bone \ but molded intentionally from dirt and stone

a world for women centers women \ decenters man

defers bodily decisions to women \ as a given

values the feminine \ without demonizing the masculine

celebrates the living \ treats wars with derision

i want to live in a world

for women \ by women
