Ruminations and remnants / use physics to learn physical hobbies

This might sound dumb of me, but I recently had a philosoraptor moment that I should just understand the laws of physics when learning any new physical activity, bc they all follow physics!

For example, when leading a spin for your Follow in West Coast Swing (WCS), you want to provide movement in the opposite direction so your follow has the space and momentum to complete the spin fully. Likewise in bouldering, some holds will require you to "flag" your body--e.g. use a leg to counterbalance your weight in the opposite direction of your hold--so that you can reach it with some stability. It's all just vectors!

The world is math. And I wish I were better at it.
