My Experience

For the past 8 years, I have been delivering digital transformation projects in the B2B SaaS, startup, and government spaces. I've led teams to build products for onboarding, education, and healthcare providers. I'm passionate about delivering the right solutions for the right users, which can only be done through discovery via live interviews, the right data, and a collaborative environment—to start.

My white collar career began with web development then full-stack software engineering building applications for businesses. As a product management consultant at Macedon and Attain, I wore many hats, leading developers, managing stakeholders, and interviewing users to prioritize and iteratively build products with maximum impact.

Wanting more ownership, I transitioned into working as a product manager at startups to help build products from 0 to 1. At Class, I interviewed customers weekly and shipped new features that helped reach 30% of revenue targets and created the product strategy and roadmap for the corporate vertical comprising Fortune 100 companies. At Dandy, I spoke with dentists and customer service reps to reduce inbound from onboarding dental practices by 97.5%. I improved On-Time Delivery estimates by 50%, working cross-functionally with data scientists, engineers, and operations executives.
